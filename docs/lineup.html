For the coordinator

The Block, your portal

Where a coordinator signs in to see every agent and what it's working on, open their dashboards and ask for help. Approvals and texts move in here next.

  • Your team roster: every rep and tier in one place, and the agents read it from there
  • A ping on your phone when an agent is stuck or waiting on you
  • One settings file per division: time zone, Vector week, texting hours, who approves
  • Each agent's rules set from a form, or drafted from your policy doc
  • An events line in your name for texts to reps

How they're run

They ask before they act

Nothing new goes out without a yesThe first time an agent sends something, you see every recipient and every word.
When in doubt, it stopsIf a number doesn't tie out or a page won't load, it sends nothing and says why.
Every agent has its own off switchPausing one never touches the others.
It proves it workedForms, emails and texts are read back before an agent calls it done.
